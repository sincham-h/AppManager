# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-03**

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application (the "Application") developed and provided by the developer ("Service Provider").

---

## 1. Overview

The Application is designed to display information about applications installed on your Android device.
All processing is performed locally on your device.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

The Application accesses information about installed applications on your device (such as app names and package identifiers) in order to display them within the app.

* This information is **processed locally on your device only**
* The Application **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data externally**
* The Application **does not access the internet**

The permission `android.permission.QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` is used solely for the purpose of retrieving a list of installed applications on the device.

---

## 3. Data Sharing

The Application does **not share any data** with third parties.

* No data is sent outside of your device
* No data is shared with other applications
* No third-party services are used

---

## 4. Data Storage

The Application does **not store any user data** outside of the device.
All accessed information is used temporarily for display purposes only.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Application does **not use any third-party services**.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect or process personal data from children under the age of 13.

---

## 7. Security

Since the Application does not transmit or store user data externally, risks associated with data leakage are minimized.
